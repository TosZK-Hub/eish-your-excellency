# Credits

Big 5 is an original lodge pet. Characters, rooms, dock icons, meter icons, and food icons are drawn for this project. No AI-generated images are used.

## Kenney (CC0 1.0)

UI buttons, the coin and star and audio glyphs, and the particle sprites are from Kenney, used under CC0. They were recolored and, for particles, scaled down so they sit in the Bushveld Pop palette.

- UI Pack, gloss and depth buttons: https://kenney.nl/assets/ui-pack
- Game Icons and Game Icons Expansion (coin, star, audio, trophy and others kept for the mask set): https://kenney.nl/assets/game-icons
- Particle Pack (star, spark, circle, twirl): https://kenney.nl/assets/particle-pack
- Files were fetched from the community mirror https://github.com/shorepine/kenney (same CC0 art, pinned paths under `ui/UI Pack`, `icons/Game Icons`, `icons/Game Icons Expansion`, and `2d/Particle Pack`).

Credit is not required by CC0. It is recorded here anyway.

## Fonts (SIL Open Font License 1.1)

- Fredoka, latin 600 and 700, self-hosted. https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Fredoka
- Nunito, latin 700 and 800, self-hosted. https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Nunito

License texts: `assets/fonts/OFL-Fredoka.txt` and `assets/fonts/OFL-Nunito.txt`.

The woff2 files are the latin subsets published by Fontsource (https://fontsource.org/), which redistributes these OFL fonts.

## Sound

Effects are synthesized in the browser with the Web Audio API. No audio files are shipped.

## Care loop

The save, drag-to-feed, bath, scoop, sleep, load-shedding, shop, daily streak, and mini-game session shape follow the working toy in `tokoloshe/` in this repository. That folder was not modified. The art, copy, animals, and games here are new.
