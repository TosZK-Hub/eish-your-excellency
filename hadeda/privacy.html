Hadeda Flap

Privacy

Hadeda Flap does not collect personal data. There is no account, no analytics SDK, and no advert network in this build.

Best score, coins, unlocked looks, and settings are stored only in local storage on this device. Reset progress in Settings deletes that data. Uninstalling the app deletes it too.

The rewarded buttons are local stand-ins. They do not contact an advert service and they do not send a device identifier.

If a later version adds AdMob, this page will be updated before that version ships, and the Play data-safety form will be updated with it.
