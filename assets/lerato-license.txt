The body, face, eyes and skeleton are from Quaternius Universal Base Characters
(standard / free pack), CC0 1.0 Universal.
https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/
https://quaternius.com/packs/universalbasecharacters.html
Models by Quaternius. The pack was claimed at the publisher's free price on
https://quaternius.itch.io/universal-base-characters

The jacket, trousers, sneakers and hair cap are new meshes made in Blender on
that skeleton (tools/dress-lerato.py). The run, idle, jump and slide clips were
keyed in that same script. Those additions are original to this project.
